## Key Takeaways

- There is no safe universal “China medical visa checklist.” The responsible embassy, consulate or visa centre decides the category and documents for that applicant.
- Obtain a truthful hospital letter, but keep its scope narrow: patient identity, department, purpose, anticipated dates and contact. It cannot guarantee a visa or final treatment.
- The patient and each companion apply in their own name. Kinship, finance and hospital companion letters may be required by some posts but not others.
- Read four fields separately: application timing, visa validity for entry, number of entries and permitted duration of each stay.
- If care might outlast the authorised stay, contact the local exit-entry administration early. An extension is a new decision, not an automatic hospital service.

## Content

A hospital can agree to review a case and a visa can still be refused. A visa can be issued and the hospital can still change the clinical plan after examination. These are **two approvals made by different authorities on different evidence**.

The most useful visa plan therefore does not promise a particular category. It creates a clean evidence chain from the declared purpose of travel to the hospital documents, finances, dates and companion arrangements—and leaves enough time for the consular authority to decide.

### Identify the responsible post first

Start with nationality, current country of legal residence, passport type and the place where the application must be lodged. If applying outside the country of nationality, proof of lawful residence may be required. The Chinese Visa Application Service Center's basic list includes a passport, application form/photo, local legal-stay evidence where applicable and prior-passport or name-change material in relevant cases [1].

Use only the current page for the responsible embassy, consulate or authorised visa centre. Requirements published for another country are useful examples, not transferable rules. Appointment method, biometrics, fees, processing options and supplementary evidence can differ.

### Do not shop for a category by nickname

“Medical visa” is conversational shorthand, not enough to complete an application. Current official guidance from some Chinese missions treats short-term medical treatment or accompanying a patient under S2/private-affairs arrangements, but the supporting documents are post-specific [2]. The national application form itself includes medical treatment among “other personal matters” [3].

That does not allow a website, hospital or facilitator to assign the final category. Describe the real activity, expected length, patient/companion role and inviting institution. Ask the responsible post which category and option to select.

Visa-free entry or transit rules should not be stretched to fit a planned treatment stay. Eligibility, permitted area, onward-ticket conditions and allowed activities are separate from the hospital plan. Confirm the exact policy for the passport and itinerary before relying on it.

### Make the hospital letter verifiable

Ask the hospital's authorised office—not an unrelated broker—to issue or confirm any required invitation, pre-admission or hospitalisation document. Ideally it should contain:

- patient name exactly as in passport, date of birth, nationality and passport number;
- hospital legal name, campus, department and official contact;
- purpose and current status: consultation, evaluation, planned admission or ongoing treatment;
- anticipated arrival and clinical window, expressed as an estimate;
- companion name and role only if the hospital can truthfully confirm it;
- issue date, signer/office and a verification route.

The letter should not claim that treatment is guaranteed, medically successful, fully prepaid or completed by a fixed date unless the hospital is authorised and able to substantiate that statement. Keep clinical detail to what the application requires; do not attach a full pathology archive to a general visa inbox.

### Keep the five evidence sets consistent

| Evidence set | What must agree |
|---|---|
| identity | passport name/number, prior names and application |
| purpose | application answers, hospital letter and itinerary |
| dates | intended entry, appointment window, accommodation and return plan |
| funding | bank/insurance/sponsor evidence and estimated costs |
| companion | separate passport/application, relationship and hospital role |

Some missions publish specific financial thresholds or companion limits for medical applications; others request different evidence. Those local rules must not be presented as a worldwide China requirement. For example, current Pakistan mission guidance and other mission pages show medical-treatment requirements under S2, but applicants elsewhere must use their own post's instructions [2].

Never edit a hospital letter, recycle another patient's letter or invent a relationship. If dates change after issuance, ask whether the hospital should reissue the document and whether the post needs the update.

### Understand the four dates printed around a visa

#### 1. Application date

This is when the post accepts the file, not a promise that a decision will arrive by the flight date. Allow for appointments, supplementary documents, passport submission and public holidays.

#### 2. Validity for entry

This is the period during which an entry may be made. It is not the permitted length of stay after arrival.

#### 3. Number of entries

A single-entry visa may be consumed after entry. A planned side trip outside mainland China can therefore break a treatment schedule. Hong Kong and Macao operate separate entry systems; do not assume a mainland visa covers them or preserves re-entry.

#### 4. Duration of each stay

Chinese regulations define duration of stay as the authorised time following each entry, counted from the day after entry [4]. Calculate the departure deadline from the actual entry and the wording on the visa or entry permission, not from the hospital's estimated discharge date.

Build a one-page date sheet with visa validity, entries, stay duration, planned arrival, accommodation, appointment, likely procedure, local recovery, last safe application date for any extension, and booked departure. Have the patient—not only the coordinator—read it.

### Apply for companions as separate travellers

A caregiver's application is not an attachment that automatically follows the patient. Confirm whether the post requests proof of relationship, a hospital-issued companion statement, shared funding or separate accommodation. Use the companion's actual role; “medical escort,” relative, interpreter and paid carer are not interchangeable.

Also plan what happens if the patient is approved but the companion is not, or if their permitted stays differ. Hospital consent and record-access authorisations are separate from immigration documents.

### Do not lock the trip before the passport returns

Keep flights and accommodation changeable until the visa is issued and the passport is physically available. A receipt, preliminary online approval or application appointment is not an entry document. The hospital should also avoid fixing a procedure whose safe timing depends on examination, tests or pathology review.

Create a contingency table for delayed decision, supplementary document request, changed appointment, passport held for processing, and visa refusal. Specify who contacts the post, hospital, airline, insurer and accommodation provider; do not ask a clinician to solve an immigration delay.

### On arrival, check the stay—not only the stamp

Confirm the entry date and permitted stay immediately. Hotels normally handle accommodation registration. A foreigner staying outside a hotel must complete local accommodation registration within 24 hours, personally or through the host, under National Immigration Administration guidance [5]. Keep the registration record if the local process provides one.

Carry passport or lawful travel/stay documentation as required and keep secure copies. A hospital admission does not suspend immigration deadlines.

### If treatment runs longer, act before the deadline

National Immigration Administration guidance says an ordinary-visa holder seeking an extension should apply to the competent local exit-entry administration and generally do so seven days before the authorised stay expires, with passport, application/photo and evidence supporting the reason [6]. Use the local office's current instructions and appointment channel [7].

An extension can be approved, shortened or refused. The revised national regulation states that an extension applies only to the current entry and does not change entry count or entry validity; cumulative extension is capped by the original authorised duration [4]. Do not promise that a new operation, complication or delayed pathology automatically produces extra time.

Ask what happens to the passport during processing and whether the receipt is sufficient for local needs. It may evidence lawful stay while the passport is held, but it should not be assumed to permit air travel, hotel check-in everywhere or cross-border movement.

### Close the immigration file at discharge

Before leaving China, reconcile passport, visa/stay document, final departure deadline, tickets and any extension receipt. If a patient's condition affects flying, solve the medical and airline questions separately; immigration permission is not fitness to fly.

Store the hospital's final records apart from the visa application. The consular file proved purpose at entry; the clinical file must accurately show what actually happened.

**Legal and medical disclaimer:** Visa and entry rules change and differ by nationality, residence, post and individual facts. This article is planning information, not legal advice or a guarantee of visa issuance, entry, hospital acceptance or treatment duration. Confirm the current case with the responsible Chinese authority and hospital.

## FAQ

### Which Chinese visa should a patient apply for?

Only the responsible Chinese embassy, consulate or authorised visa centre can confirm the category for that applicant. Some current mission guidance uses S2/private-affairs pathways for medical treatment, but documents and options vary by post.

### Is a hospital invitation letter enough to obtain a visa?

No. It supports purpose but does not replace the application, passport, identity, finance, residence, companion or other evidence requested by the deciding authority.

### Can a companion use the patient's approval?

No. Each traveller needs their own lawful basis and documents. Some posts ask for kinship and hospital companion proof; check the companion's application separately.

### Can a hospital extend the patient's visa after surgery?

The hospital may provide medical evidence, but the competent exit-entry administration decides. Apply before expiry and plan for refusal or a shorter period than requested.

### Does visa validity show how long I may remain in China?

No. Entry validity, number of entries and duration of each stay are separate fields. Calculate the lawful departure date from the actual entry and authorised stay.
